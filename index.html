How to Play

You get a start word and a target word, both the same length.

Change exactly one letter at a time to build a ladder of valid English words, until you reach the target.

  • Every word you add must be a real word from the WordBound word list.
  • You can't repeat a word already on your ladder.
  • Letters can't be added or removed. Every row has the same length.
  • You have a few free hints. Each one costs 10 points. The first hint is vague, the second reveals a letter, and the third reveals the next word.
  • Fewer moves, no hints, and finishing fast all raise your score.

Daily Easy and Daily Hard give everyone the same puzzle each day. Unlimited gives you a new random puzzle every time.
